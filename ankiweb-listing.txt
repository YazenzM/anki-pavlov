TITLE
Pavlov: Photo Rewards

TAGS
reward motivation photo image gamification

DESCRIPTION
Reward yourself with your own photos for getting cards right.

Each time you press Hard, Good or Easy, Pavlov shows a random photo from a folder you choose: holiday snaps, pets, family, anything you enjoy. A small, personal reward gives you something to look forward to in every review session.

FEATURES
- Use any folder on your computer, including Google Drive (through Google Drive for Desktop).
- Put the photo in a corner or a side panel so it doesn't block your next card, or show it full screen.
- Choose the photo size, how long it stays up, which answer buttons earn one, and how often it appears. Set a chance below 100% for surprise rewards.
- Every photo is shown once before any repeats.
- Works on AnkiMobile and AnkiDroid too (see below).

GETTING STARTED
1. Tools > Pavlov > Settings... and choose a photo folder.
2. Study as usual. Click a photo to close it early.

PHONES AND TABLETS
AnkiMobile and AnkiDroid can't run add-ons. On your computer, use Tools > Pavlov > Copy Photos to Phone/Tablet..., then sync. A random photo then appears each time you reveal an answer on your phone or tablet. Phone and tablet apps don't say which button you pressed, so the photo shows whether or not you got the card right.

This adds a small script to the back of your card types. Tools > Pavlov > Remove from Phone/Tablet... removes it again.

SETTINGS
Tools > Pavlov > Settings...

Supported formats: JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF (first frame). HEIC isn't supported.

Bug reports and source code: https://github.com/YazenzM/anki-pavlov
